Drill Name: Chemical Spill Response
Drill ID: DRILL_03 (chem-v1)

Category: Hazmat
Difficulty Level: Advanced
Duration: 20 min

Purpose:
Immediate containment procedures for industrial solvents, acid line breaches, and toxic liquid spill containment zones.

Required PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) / Equipment involved:
- Full Face Shield
- Heavy Nitrile Gloves
- Chemical Apron
- Vapor Respirator

Step-by-step procedure:
1. Cordon & Evacuate: Flag 10-meter perimeter boundary and alert downwind plant personnel immediately.
2. Consult SDS Placard: Scan the drum barcode with AR to identify pH, vapor density, and reactive risks.
3. Deploy Absorbent Booms: Encircle outer perimeter of the spill liquid to block drainage inlet runoff.

Knowledge check questions and correct answers (for the worker's understanding):
Q: What is the first step when a hazardous chemical spill occurs?
A: Evacuate and secure the perimeter
Q: Where should you place absorbent booms?
A: Around the outer perimeter
Q: What document must you check to understand the chemical's reactive risks?
A: Safety Data Sheet (SDS)
Q: Which PPE is generally required for an unknown acidic spill?
A: Level B Hazmat suit

Scoring:
Workers are scored on correct completion of each step in the procedure above, and on their answers to the knowledge check questions. Total: 100 points. Passing score is 70.
