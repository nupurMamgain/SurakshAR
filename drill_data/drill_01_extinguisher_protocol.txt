Drill Name: Extinguisher Protocol
Drill ID: DRILL_01 (fire-v1)

Category: Fire
Difficulty Level: Beginner
Duration: 15 min

Purpose:
Master the mandatory industrial standard P.A.S.S. protocol on electrical and fuel flare hazards in confined work zones.

Required PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) / Equipment involved:
- Heat-Resistant Gloves
- Safety Goggles
- Fire-Retardant Coverall

Step-by-step procedure:
1. Identify Fire Hazard: Center the target on the combustion source to distinguish Class A vs Class C flame.
2. Select Extinguisher: Confirm CO2 canister selection on the tool wheel.
3. Pull Safety Pin: Execute the pin break sequence to disengage the locking ring.
4. Aim At Base: Hold reticle at the base of the virtual fire, avoiding the smoke plume.
5. Sweep Motion: Pan camera side-to-side to extinguish the hazard.

Knowledge check questions and correct answers (for the worker's understanding):
Q: What does the 'A' in the P.A.S.S. protocol stand for?
A: Aim at the base
Q: Which extinguisher type should NEVER be used on an electrical fire?
A: Water
Q: How far away should you stand when initially discharging an extinguisher?
A: 8-10 feet
Q: What should you do if the fire continues to grow after using one full extinguisher?
A: Evacuate immediately
Q: What visual indicator shows that a fire extinguisher is fully pressurized?
A: The gauge needle is in the green zone

Scoring:
Workers are scored on correct completion of each step in the procedure above, and on their answers to the knowledge check questions. Total: 100 points. Passing score is 70.
