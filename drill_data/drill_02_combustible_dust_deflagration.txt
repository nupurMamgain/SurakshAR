Drill Name: Combustible Dust Deflagration
Drill ID: DRILL_02 (fire-v2)

Category: Fire
Difficulty Level: Advanced
Duration: 22 min

Purpose:
Identify and mitigate combustible dust accumulation on factory rafters before a secondary explosion sequence triggers.

Required PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) / Equipment involved:
- Nomex Flash Suit
- Anti-Static Footwear
- Full-Face Respirator

Step-by-step procedure:
1. Detect Dust Cloud: Use AR thermal mode to identify suspended particulate density above LEL limits.
2. Kill Ignition Sources: Disable nearby welding arcs and non-intrinsically safe electrical motors.
3. Activate Exhaust: Engage the local explosion-proof exhaust ventilation system.

Knowledge check questions and correct answers (for the worker's understanding):
Q: What is the most dangerous consequence of disturbing settled combustible dust?
A: Secondary explosion
Q: Which cleaning method should be STRICTLY avoided in a dust-heavy environment?
A: Using compressed air
Q: What type of electrical equipment must be used in a combustible dust area?
A: Intrinsically safe / Explosion-proof
Q: How should water be applied to a combustible dust fire?
A: Low-pressure fog
Q: What does 'LEL' stand for in gas and dust monitoring?
A: Lower Explosive Limit

Scoring:
Workers are scored on correct completion of each step in the procedure above, and on their answers to the knowledge check questions. Total: 100 points. Passing score is 70.
