Drill Name: Confined Space Gas Leak
Drill ID: DRILL_04 (chem-v2)

Category: Hazmat
Difficulty Level: Intermediate
Duration: 25 min

Purpose:
Protocol for entering subterranean vaults with suspected Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S) displacement and oxygen deprivation.

Required PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) / Equipment involved:
- 4-Gas Monitor
- SCBA Tank
- Extraction Harness

Step-by-step procedure:
1. Deploy Gas Sniffer: Drop the 4-gas monitor probe into the vault and wait 60 seconds for AR readout.
2. Engage Blower: Position the positive-pressure ventilation tube into the space to clear toxic vapor.
3. Rig Tripod Hoist: Set up the extraction winch directly over the manhole opening.

Knowledge check questions and correct answers (for the worker's understanding):
Q: What device is absolutely required before entering a confined space?
A: 4-Gas Monitor
Q: What gas smells like 'rotten eggs' at low concentrations but deadens your sense of smell at high levels?
A: Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S)
Q: What is the minimum acceptable oxygen level for entering a confined space without supplied air?
A: 19.5%
Q: What should an attendant do if the entrant stops responding?
A: Initiate non-entry rescue using the tripod hoist

Scoring:
Workers are scored on correct completion of each step in the procedure above, and on their answers to the knowledge check questions. Total: 100 points. Passing score is 70.
