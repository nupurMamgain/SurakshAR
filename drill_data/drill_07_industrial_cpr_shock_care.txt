Drill Name: Industrial CPR & Shock Care
Drill ID: DRILL_07 (firstaid-v1)

Category: First Aid
Difficulty Level: Beginner
Duration: 12 min

Purpose:
Emergency basic life support, CPR cadence synchronization, and automated external defibrillator (AED) pad setup on site.

Required PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) / Equipment involved:
- Nitrile Exam Gloves
- CPR Pocket Mask

Step-by-step procedure:
1. Survey Scene Safety: Inspect floor for hanging wires, slippery oil pools, or continuing hazard leaks.
2. Begin Chest Compressions: Place palm center of sternum, compress 2 inches deep at 100-120 BPM tempo.
3. Stand Clear & Shock Delivery: Ensure zero worker contact during automated heart rhythm analysis & shock cycle.

Knowledge check questions and correct answers (for the worker's understanding):
Q: What is the correct depth and rate for adult chest compressions?
A: 2 inches at 100-120 BPM
Q: What is the first thing you should do when finding an unconscious worker?
A: Survey the scene for hazards
Q: When using an AED, what must you ensure before pressing the 'Shock' button?
A: Everyone is standing clear of the victim
Q: If a worker is in shock (pale, cold, rapid pulse), what is the best initial positioning?
A: Lay them flat and elevate their legs

Scoring:
Workers are scored on correct completion of each step in the procedure above, and on their answers to the knowledge check questions. Total: 100 points. Passing score is 70.
