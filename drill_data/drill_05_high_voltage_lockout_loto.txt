Drill Name: High-Voltage Lockout (LOTO)
Drill ID: DRILL_05 (elec-v1)

Category: Electrical
Difficulty Level: Intermediate
Duration: 18 min

Purpose:
Zero-energy state verification and standardized Lockout/Tagout isolation for plant motor control distribution centers.

Required PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) / Equipment involved:
- Class 0 Insulated Gloves
- Arc Flash Hood
- Nonconductive Boots

Step-by-step procedure:
1. Trip Main Isolator: Open breaker switch cleanly with single swift motion using insulated glove.
2. Apply Physical Lock & Hasp: Secure OSHA-compliant red master padlock through isolation switch clamp.
3. Verify Zero Energy State: Use calibrated multimeter probes on all 3 phases to confirm zero residual charge.

Knowledge check questions and correct answers (for the worker's understanding):
Q: Who is authorized to remove a Lockout tag and padlock?
A: Only the worker who applied it
Q: What must be done immediately after applying a lock and tag?
A: Attempt to start the machine to verify zero energy
Q: When verifying a zero-energy state with a multimeter, what is the correct testing sequence?
A: Test known live source, test target, test known live source again
Q: Why are residual capacitors dangerous even after the main power is cut?
A: They store high voltage charges that must be grounded

Scoring:
Workers are scored on correct completion of each step in the procedure above, and on their answers to the knowledge check questions. Total: 100 points. Passing score is 70.
