Drill Name: Transformer Arc Flash Defense
Drill ID: DRILL_06 (elec-v2)

Category: Electrical
Difficulty Level: Advanced
Duration: 30 min

Purpose:
Establish approach boundaries and execute emergency disconnects on a failing 13.8kV plant step-down transformer.

Required PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) / Equipment involved:
- 40 Cal/cm² Arc Flash Suit
- Dielectric Overshoes
- Hot Stick

Step-by-step procedure:
1. Establish Boundary: Mark the 10-foot limited approach boundary with red barricade tape.
2. Inspect Transformer: Use AR thermal imaging to locate the overheating bushing.
3. Operate Hot Stick: Use a 6-foot fiberglass hot stick to open the primary cutout switch from a distance.

Knowledge check questions and correct answers (for the worker's understanding):
Q: What tool must be used to open a high-voltage disconnect switch?
A: Fiberglass Hot Stick
Q: What does 'cal/cm²' measure on Arc Flash PPE?
A: Thermal incident energy rating
Q: Before opening a transformer enclosure, what must be applied?
A: Temporary grounding clamps
Q: True or False: Synthetic clothing like nylon or polyester is safe to wear under an arc flash suit.
A: False - they can melt to the skin

Scoring:
Workers are scored on correct completion of each step in the procedure above, and on their answers to the knowledge check questions. Total: 100 points. Passing score is 70.
