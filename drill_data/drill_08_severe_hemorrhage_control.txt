Drill Name: Severe Hemorrhage Control
Drill ID: DRILL_08 (firstaid-v2)

Category: First Aid
Difficulty Level: Intermediate
Duration: 15 min

Purpose:
Rapid response protocol for severe arterial bleeding caused by heavy machinery entanglement or crushing incidents.

Required PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) / Equipment involved:
- Nitrile Exam Gloves
- Safety Glasses

Step-by-step procedure:
1. Isolate Machinery: Hit the emergency E-Stop button on the offending machinery before approaching.
2. Apply Direct Pressure: Pack the wound with hemostatic gauze and apply heavy downward bodyweight pressure.
3. Apply Tourniquet: Place tourniquet 2 inches above the wound; twist the windlass until bleeding stops completely.

Knowledge check questions and correct answers (for the worker's understanding):
Q: Where should a tourniquet be applied relative to a bleeding wound?
A: 2 inches above the wound
Q: What should you do before approaching a worker caught in machinery?
A: Hit the emergency E-Stop button
Q: When applying a tourniquet, how tight should the windlass be twisted?
A: Until the arterial bleeding completely stops
Q: After applying a tourniquet, what critical information must be recorded?
A: The exact time of application

Scoring:
Workers are scored on correct completion of each step in the procedure above, and on their answers to the knowledge check questions. Total: 100 points. Passing score is 70.
